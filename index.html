Our Story

Built From a Simple Conviction

Walk into most Nigerian furniture stores, and you’ll find one of two things: cheap imports that fall apart in two harmattan seasons, or luxury pieces priced so high they feel like they belong to someone else’s life. We started TrIM² because we believed there was a third option. Furniture that is genuinely beautiful, genuinely durable, and genuinely within reach.

We build from premium African hardwoods — iroko, sapele, walnut — materials that have been growing on this continent for decades before they reach our workshop. We use traditional joinery techniques passed down through generations, combined with contemporary design that fits how Nigerians actually live today. Not how a European catalogue imagines we live. How we actually live.

“The wood remembers the hands that shaped it.”

But TrIM² was never just about furniture. From the beginning we believed that the greatest resource in Nigerian craft is the young person who hasn’t been given a chance to develop a skill yet. Every commission we take funds a training seat. Every table we build teaches someone how to build the next one. That is the company we are building — one piece at a time.

The TrIM² Way

Good Furniture.
Honest Process.
Real People.

We don’t have a complicated system. We have a simple promise — show up, listen properly, build well, and never cut a corner the client can’t see.

01

Design With You

We begin every project with a real conversation. Not a form, not a price list. We visit your space, ask how you actually use it, and design around your life — not around what’s easiest for us to make. No two commissions leave our workshop looking the same.

02

Made

Every piece is cut, joined, finished, and inspected here. By Nigerian hands. With Nigerian wood. We don’t outsource, we don’t import parts, and we don’t rush. When your piece is being built, we’ll send you progress updates. You can visit. Most clients do.

03

Delivered & Placed

We don’t leave your furniture at the gate. We bring it in, place it where it belongs, and don’t leave until it looks like it was always there. Delivery includes full installation and a care guide so the piece lasts as long as it should — which is decades.

And while we’re doing all of this — every commission, every delivery, every satisfied client — we are simultaneously building something much bigger.
