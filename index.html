Our Story

Built From a Simple Conviction

Walk into most Nigerian furniture shops and you see two choices: cheap imports that fail after a few harmattan seasons, or luxury pieces priced out of reach. TRIM² exists for the third option. We make furniture that is beautiful, built to last, and priced for real homes.

We work with premium African hardwoods like iroko and sapele and use traditional joinery alongside contemporary design that fits how Nigerians actually live. Every piece is made in our workshop with care and respect for the material.

“The wood remembers the hands that shaped it.”

TRIM² is also a training ground. Every commission helps fund a training seat so young makers can learn a trade and build a future. One piece, one skill, one community at a time.

The TrIM² Way

Real.
Aesthetic.

We don’t have a complicated system. We have a simple promise, we show up, listen, build well, and never cut a corner the client can’t see.

01

Design With You

We begin every project with a real conversation. Not a form, not a price list. We visit your space, ask how you actually use it, and design around your life, not around what’s easiest for us to make. No two commissions leave our workshop looking the same.

02

Made

Every piece is cut, joined, finished, and inspected here. By Nigerian hands. With Nigerian wood. We don’t outsource, we don’t import parts, and we don’t rush. When your piece is being built, we’ll send you progress updates. You can visit. Most clients do.

03

Delivered & Placed

We don’t leave your furniture at the gate. We bring it in, place it where it belongs, and don’t leave until it looks like it was always there. Delivery includes full installation and a care guide so the piece lasts as long as it should — which is decades.

And while we’re doing all of this, we are simultaneously building something much bigger.
